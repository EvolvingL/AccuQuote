Questions

Before you sign up.

Is it really free for life?

Yes. Everyone who joins the pilot and helps us test keeps free access to AccuQuote for life. You won’t be asked for a card, and nothing turns into a paid plan without you choosing it.

What’s the catch?

We need tradespeople using it on real jobs and telling us where it falls short. That feedback is worth more to us right now than a subscription fee.

Which phone do I need?

An iPhone. Room scanning works on any iPhone running iOS 15 or later. iPhone 12 Pro and later Pro models have LiDAR, which gives faster and more accurate scans and unlocks Space mode for tight spots like under-sink voids. On other iPhones you can sweep the camera or type in tape-measure readings.

Where do the prices come from?

From our approved Travis Perkins data feed, refreshed daily. Every material on your quote is priced at what it costs at Travis Perkins now.

What will you do with my email?

We’ll send your download link, the first-scan guide and occasional pilot updates. We never sell or share it, and you can unsubscribe from any email in one click. See our privacy policy.

When do I get the instructions?

Straight away. The email usually arrives within a couple of minutes. If it hasn’t, check your spam or promotions folder.
