3. The service

AccuQuote is a mobile application that lets UK tradespeople scan a room (using their device's camera and, where available, LiDAR sensor), describe a job, and receive an AI-generated itemised quote covering labour and materials. Where a live supplier pricing feed is available for a given material, we use that; otherwise pricing is estimated using AI and clearly marked as such. The app also allows users to export quotes as PDF documents, request deposit payments from their own customers via Stripe, and save quote history and 3D scan data.

We reserve the right to add, change, or remove features of the service at any time, and to release new versions of the app that supersede earlier ones.

5. AI-generated quotes & pricing accuracy

This is the most important section of these terms for how you actually use the app — please read it carefully.

  • Quotes, materials lists, labour estimates, room dimensions, and prices generated by AccuQuote are produced using artificial intelligence (Anthropic's Claude models) combined with, where available, live supplier pricing data. They are estimates, not guarantees.
  • Where a specific supplier's live pricing feed is available for a material, the quote will indicate this ("real" pricing). Where no live feed is available, pricing is an AI-generated estimate based on general market rates and is clearly marked as an estimate. You should treat estimated prices as a starting point, not a final figure.
  • Room dimensions produced by LiDAR scanning are generally accurate to within a few centimetres under good conditions, but accuracy can be affected by scan quality, room complexity, reflective or transparent surfaces, and device limitations. Non-LiDAR "camera sweep" or manual entry methods are less precise.
  • You are responsible for reviewing every quote before sending it to your customer. AccuQuote is a tool to speed up and assist your quoting process — it does not replace your professional judgement, site knowledge, or responsibility to price a job accurately and safely. You should not send a quote you have not personally reviewed and are not prepared to honour.
  • We are not liable for losses arising from inaccuracies in AI-generated content, including but not limited to under-pricing, over-pricing, incorrect material quantities, or incorrect dimensions, except where such inaccuracy arises from our negligence and is not excluded by law (see section 15, Liability).
